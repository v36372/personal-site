## A home for writing

This website is becoming a personal blog for Tin Nguyen, based in Ho Chi Minh
City, Vietnam. The idea is simple: a quiet place for notes, essays, and things
worth keeping. The writing comes first; the little globe is a reminder of
where this corner of the internet is based.

There is no need to fill every space at once. A small site can grow one post
at a time, with a chronological archive that makes older writing easy to find.

## Keeping it simple

Posts are written in Markdown. A title, a date, and a short description give
each entry just enough context. Paragraphs have room to breathe, links look
like links, and the layout stays out of the way.

A post can include:

- Short notes or longer essays.
- Headings, lists, and quotes.
- Links to related reading and small code examples.

For example, a draft starts with a little metadata:
